Privacy Policy

Information about using this website and contacting our independent tree removal referral enquiry line.

Effective: 22 November 2026. This policy should be checked against the live call-routing and data-handling arrangements before publication.

1. Who We Are

weaverstreeservices.com is an independently operated information, lead-generation and tree removal referral website serving people making tree removal enquiries in the Pine Bluff, Arkansas area. The site is not the former Weaver’s Tree Services business, does not employ or supply tree removal crews, and does not itself perform tree removal work.

2. Information We May Collect

When you make a telephone enquiry, we may receive your name, telephone number or caller ID, city or ZIP code, details of the tree or property issue, appointment preferences, and any other information you choose to provide. Telephone systems may also process call date and time, routing, duration, outcome, and related technical records. Our hosting provider may automatically process IP addresses, browser details and ordinary website access logs.

This homepage does not currently include a web enquiry form. Please avoid submitting sensitive payment, identity, health or financial information in a voicemail.

3. Why We Use Information

Information may be used to receive and review your requested tree removal enquiry, follow up about that enquiry where appropriate, determine whether it can be referred to an independent provider, support call handling and call validation, prevent fraud and misuse, maintain service security, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Independent Providers, Lead Generation and Compensation

This is a referral and lead-generation service. Enquiries may be routed or referred to independent tree removal service providers and to technology or call-routing partners involved in handling those requests. The operator may receive compensation when a qualifying telephone enquiry is referred or otherwise meets a referral arrangement's requirements. Compensation does not guarantee that any provider will respond, quote, accept a booking or complete work.

Independent providers are responsible for their own policies and any appointments, estimates, contracts or work.

5. Telephone Tracking, Press-1 Prompts and Recordings

We may use a third-party telecommunications platform to receive, route and measure calls. Where the current arrangement invites a caller to leave a voicemail, that message will be recorded after an appropriate notice, and it may be reviewed to understand and respond to the requested enquiry. Call tracking may process the originating telephone number, call time, duration, routing and status. A prompt to press 1 may be used to confirm a caller wishes to continue.

In the future, calls may instead be routed live to independent providers and call handling or recording practices may change. We will update these disclosures and provide any notice or obtain consent required by applicable law before enabling additional recording. A telephone call alone does not grant consent to unrelated marketing calls or texts.

6. When We Share Information

Enquiry details and relevant call information may be provided to independent service providers, lead or call-routing partners, telecommunications suppliers, and service providers who help operate the website. Information may also be shared where reasonably required to address fraud or security problems or comply with law.

7. Website Cookies and Technical Logs

The supplied homepage does not set custom advertising cookies or include an analytics tracking pixel. Ordinary hosting and network providers may maintain technical logs for delivery and security. If analytics, advertising pixels or non-essential cookies are later added, this policy and any required notices should be revised before use.

8. Retention and Security

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for enquiry handling, call validation, recordkeeping, dispute resolution or legal obligations, then securely deleted or de-identified where appropriate. The precise retention settings of connected telecommunications and referral providers must be checked before launch. No transmission or storage system is perfectly secure.

9. Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information or raise concerns about its handling. To make a privacy enquiry, email dean AT djfpublishing DOT com (replace AT with @ and DOT with .) and include the website address. We may need to verify your request. Independent service providers may separately control information they receive.

10. Children and Third-Party Sites

The website is intended for adults seeking property services. We do not knowingly seek information from children. Independent businesses and third-party sites may follow their own privacy notices and terms.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, referral providers or telephone practices change. The latest version will be published here.
